Story of the Game

Hundreds of years ago, there lived a pirate who had just turned forty.
But that night... Death came for him.
Desperate to escape his fate, he made a pact with Death.
He would never grow old. He would never die.
But there was a price.
He would live alone..forever..on a desolate island.
His only companion would be his gun. And for every year he remained alive, a living soul would die.
Years passed. Finally, he grew tired of the solitude. So he decided to set sail across the endless ocean determined to find someone...
But he was forbidden to leave the island . The moment he tried to build a ship, he broke the pact.
Then, through the mist, he saw a fleet of ships approaching.
But there was no crew aboard, only - the DEAD. And they had come for him...

About Game

The Pact is an immersive pirate themed tower defense roguelite developed in Unreal Engine 5.1.

The number of waves varies from 5 to 18 depending on the selected level. The game includes an in game market where you can purchase items and upgrade your towers, guns, and items.

This project was initially created as a tutorial project inspired by the YouTube video by Smart Poly.
Video link: https://youtu.be/xCf3SEOEDAo?si=S5QlYFVtzBxIXQQ2

While the visual style and core setup build upon the tutorial, I have extensively refactored and expanded the codebase.
My Personal Contributions and Improvements:
Custom Blueprint logic for advanced wave mechanics | Different types of enemies with custom behaviors | Upgrade logic for weapons and tower | Enhanced item placement system and configurable settings | Complete game balancing, including enemy power and pricing for upgrades and items | Slot Machine and its entire underlying logic | Various other details and quality of life improvements

Note: AI was used strictly as a learning aid and for generating artwork. All game logic and systems were created entirely by me.
